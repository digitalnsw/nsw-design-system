Easy Read: How to report scams and cybercrime

This page tells you what to do if you have been scammed.

On this page

  • What scams and cybercrime are
  • Stop contacting the scammer
  • Contact your bank
  • Change your passwords
  • Write down the scam details
  • Report the scam
  • Report cybercrime online
  • Get support

What scams and cybercrime are

A scam is when someone tries to trick you.

They might try to take your money.

A cybercrime is when someone uses a computer or the internet to break the law.

Stop contacting the scammer

A scammer is a person who does a scam.

Stop calling or messaging the scammer.

Do not send them more money.

Contact your bank

Contact your bank if the scammer has your bank details.

Your bank can help protect your account and cards.

Change your passwords

Change the passwords for your devices.

Change the passwords for your online accounts.

Use a different password for each account.

Write down the scam details

Write down information about the scammer.

This might include their phone number, email address or website.

This information can help stop the scammer.

Report the scam

Report the scam as soon as you can.

Call NSW Fair Trading on 13 32 20 if the scam started in NSW.

You can also report the scam to NSW Fair Trading online.

For scams outside NSW, report the scam to Scamwatch.

Report cybercrime online

Use ReportCyber to report a cybercrime.

ReportCyber will send your report to the right organisation.

Report a cybercrime on the ReportCyber website

Get support

Tell someone you trust what happened.

Your family, friends or support person might be able to help.

Contact ID Support NSW if someone has stolen your identity.

Your identity is the information that proves who you are.

Get help from ID Support NSW
